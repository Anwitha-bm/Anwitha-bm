### 🚀 About Me

- 🎓 **Education:** B.Tech in Electronics & Communication Engineering (Batch of 2028) at **G. Narayanamma Institute of Technology & Science (GNITS)**, minor in **Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning**.
- 🔬 **R&D Focus:** Developing **Project V.A.J.R.A 917** in collaboration with the *Silicon Labs Centre of Excellence in IoT*.
- 🛰️ **Space & Aerospace Engineering:** 
  - Built a **1U CubeSat & Telemetry Ground Station** prototype, tested sensor telemetry via aerial drone and balloon cluster deployments.

  - Designed and simulated dual-band stacked microstrip patch antenna operating at GPS frequencies using **Ansys HFSS** and **CST Studio Suite**(based on an ieee paper).

- 💻 **Software & AI:** Full-stack development with **React, Node.js, Express, Firebase**, and building generative AI audio applications using **Streamlit, Watson TTS, and Hugging Face**.
